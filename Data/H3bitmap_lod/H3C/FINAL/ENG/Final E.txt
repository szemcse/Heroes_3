===== Térkép neve =====
Secrets Revealed

===== Térkép leírása =====
Yog and Crag Hack must defeat the Necromancers to win the scenario.  However, if either hero is defeated in combat, the scenario is lost.  Yog and Crag Hack are limited to level 33 but will carry their experience, skills and spells on to their next scenario.

All you had to do was mention that if he built an army he could roll over all the undead in this area in a matter of weeks.  Finally he nodded in agreement.  At least his skull isn't completely empty, you say to yourself as he leaves the room.

Not liking this feeling, you send out some scouts to take a look at the area around you.  Hopefully they will bring back some intelligence on the enemy.

Strong Forces

Crag's Reply

Laughing to yourself, you come to the realization you were crazy to even think about joining forces with that... that... that Barbarian.  Crag's reply had come just a short while ago.  Here is what it said: "We build, we crush.  Fear not the smelly ones."  Well the message was blunt, if anything.  Realizing this Barbarian will probably rush off into battle half prepared, you bark orders to speed up the preparations.

Meeting
Üzenet:
Well, I am almost ready.  I think it's about time to set up a meeting with Crag Hack to discuss an attack plan.  Sending for a messenger, you quickly write a letter asking for a meeting within the next few weeks.

Név:
History
Üzenet:
Crag Hack showed up today, and he was eager to discuss exactly how we were going to crush the Necromancers.  He had practice in this before, explaining how he killed lots of Necromancers for some artifacts.  Those artifacts were supposed to be his ticket to gold, land and women.  But it never happened because Sandro cheated him out of the whole deal.  Now he just wants to kill stuff to relieve stress.  I laid out my plans, explaining that the longer he waited in some instances, the more necromancers he got to crush.  He seemed to agree with the idea, but I wasn't too convinced when he left.

Név:
Two Rangers
Üzenet:
Something promising reached your ears last night while you prepared for sleep.  It seems you and Crag Hack are not the only ones fighting these Necromancers.  A Ranger and a Druid, Gelu and Gem, are also fending off the forces of undead, but they are working within the borders of AvLee.  You had not realized the Necromancer's influence reached so far.  Quickly, you pen a letter explaining your situation and send it off to them, hoping they will meet with you at a later date.

Név:
Gem and Gelu
Üzenet:
A messenger arrived shortly ago informing me that Gem and Gelu want to meet in Bragden, which is in the Northwestern area of Deyja.  "Well," you think to yourself, "as soon as we get done here we will be on our way."
