===== Térkép neve =====
Retrieving the Cowl

===== Térkép leírása =====
Bring the Vampire's Cowl to the town of Leafhall to win the scenario.  However, if Gem or Clancy are defeated in combat or you lose the Cowl, the scenario is lost.  Your heroes are limited to level 20, but Gem and Clancy will carry their experience, skills and spells on to the next scenario.

Intro

A Barbarian named Terek has located the Vampire's Cowl for Sandro.  Unfortunately, Bandits in the Contested Lands captured Terek on his way back from Deyja.   Terek convinced the bandits that his friends would pay handsomely for his return with his possessions intact.		My scrying has shown he is being held in an underground prison near the Deyjan border.

Another Harvested Village
Üzenet:
Today we rode through another small hamlet that had been "harvested" by a Necromancer about a month ago.  I buried what was left of the children's bodies.  There weren't any adults; they had all been raised as skeletons, I'm sure.		I feel so old and tired and angry and unsettled when I see this type of thing.  I don't see how Erathia is going to retain the Contested Lands if they can't protect their people from this horror.

Név:
Another Harvested Village (cont)
Üzenet:
I must stay focused on my quest.  I found out very painfully I couldn't single-handedly rid the world of evil.  The best I can do is help others help themselves.  I gave Mayor Delino gold to help resettle people near Clovergreen, where the militia can protect them.  I gave him the rest of my coin to help support the militia.  The militia are all volunteers, but they still need weapons, equipment and supplies.

Név:
Scouting Report Border Lord
Üzenet:
Apparently I made an impression on the Deyjan Border Lords.  My Elven scouts report the nearest Border Lord seems to be raising an army to challenge our presence near the border.  They suspect a Vampire Scout spotted us when we moved into this area of the Contested Lands.

Név:
Dream from Manda
Üzenet:
I had another dream about Amanda last night.  I think the dreams are real, and she's communicating with me by magic.		She asked me how I was faring.  I told her I had recovered the Amulet for Sandro and agreed to help his master, Ethric, find a Vampire's Cowl and a pair of Dead Man's Boots.  Shaking with fury, I also told her about the 'harvesting,' and how I was trying to help the villagers in this area.  She just looked at me with her wise, calm eyes and advised me to be careful, very careful about what I was doing, and it wouldn't be like the last time.

Név:
Sorting it out
Üzenet:
I was thinking about... things today.  Sometimes I just refuse to think about certain things, but it hurts too much, and, besides, I feel more settled now.  I don't think it's wrong to hate the Hateful or not forgive the Unforgivable.  What I think is wrong is to let that hating and unforgiving turn a person into the thing they hate or won't forgive.  I won't make that mistake again.  I won't be like them.

Név:
Visit from Lord
Üzenet:
Clancy's Lord Fayette arrived today!  What a pleasant surprise!  He claimed he was on a "Hunting Trip" and winked at me.  This is the first chance I had to talk to him, and I liked him a lot.  We talked about the forest, animals and people.  Just before he left, he said he would like for me to think about joining the AvLee forces as a hero working for either him or another AvLee Lord.  He told me to send him a messenger with my thoughts on the matter in a few days

Név:
Letter to Lord
Üzenet:
I wanted to write Lord Fayette right after his visit, but I forced myself to let some time pass.  I finally wrote him today that I would be happy to join his forces but needed to finish gathering the items Ethric wanted first.  I think he will understand.  I'll just have to wait and see.

Név:
Letter from Lord
Üzenet:
I received a letter from Lord Fayette today.  I had to open it right away.  It formally invited me to join his forces as a General as soon as my promise to Sandro was fulfilled.  He said some flattering things that made me laugh.  That elf lord is also a charmer.  It is going to be a pleasure working for him.
