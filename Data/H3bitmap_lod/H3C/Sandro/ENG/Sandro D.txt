===== Térkép neve =====
Duke Alarice

===== Térkép leírása =====
Defeat Duke Alarice and all his allies to win the campaign.  However, if Sandro or Finneas are defeated in combat, the scenario is lost.  Sandro and Finneas are limited to level 25 but will carry their experience, skills and spells on to their next campaign.
