Bidley and Jeremy were denied their revenge. A defeated Casmetra left everything behind and leapt into a portal; it collapsed right away. The witch was never to be seen in Regna again. Bidley took the Horn, yet he never found out what exactly Casmetra was trying to accomplish with it. 		After a week of fruitless search for the witch, they abandoned their attempts; for all intents and purposes, Bidley was the Pirate King now. 		At that point, Regna was not a recognized state anymore, although it was still occasionally spoken of as “Empire of the Endless Ocean”. Thing is, after the pasing of old Hareck IX, his heir failed to step forward. A power struggle commenced among pirates. In the ensuing turmoil, several captains had proved to be strong enough to seize control over vast territories, but once they perished or lost their edge, chaos resumed. 		Here and now, Bidley was the King, and this word meant not only terror and reverence, but also lots of hatred. Owning a powerful ancient artifact, he could afford to disregard his former peers, the mere seafarers; for fear of the unknown was all he felt. 		The real danger looming was beyond the seas.

It was a clear day; Bidley and his crew were in a coastal fortress. The Horn of the Abyss was habitually hanging from the Pirate King's belt; he never parted with it. Suddenly, a sailor came running. Visibly scared, he had a scroll in his hands.	“Cap’n, cap’n! I swear by all the sea devils! It was a genie! A genie appeared and told me to deliver this to you!” Dazed, Bidley took the scroll and unraveled it. 	The message read: “I salute you, Bidley Roberts, the new Pirate King! 		I am Solmyr, a modest servant of Gavin Magnus, the Immortal King of Bracada. My lord is disquieted by the news that you are now in the possession of the legendary artifact, the Horn of the Abyss. We reckon that this item must be kept here, in Bracada, under a close watch of the most powerful archmages. You are not a man of science; rather, a bandit and a thief. No doubt your use of the artifact will be ill-purpose: to obtain power and riches, that is. We, the mages of Bracada, cannot stay aside and watch the Horn of the Abyss fall in the hands of a reprobate like you.		Thuswise, I inform you that we are about to send an armed expedition force, with myself personally heading it. I shall not ask you to yield the artefact willingly, knowing that you will refuse in any case. I also bring it to your notice that Elemental Lords are willing to support my lord. They are equally alarmed by the situation, and the Lord of Water is enraged by your razing of the Coralgate fortress.     		To the Pirate King from Solmyr, genie of Bracada.” 		“Check out the big brain on the genie,” Bidley nodded. “Of course I won’t give up the Horn. He can come and take it.”
